Statista's latest infographic, a data chart on a different subject each
weekday. Sponsored posts are left out.

**Tap the card** for full screen with the chart's description.

## Configure

**Settings → Chart of the Day.** Choose which topics the card shows. Every
topic is on to start. The card rotates through the selected topics, moving to
the next one every 30 minutes. With no topic selected, it shows the newest
chart overall.

The topics are:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Consumer Goods
- E-Commerce
- Economy
- Energy & Environment
- Entertainment
- Finance
- Health
- Internet
- Media
- Retail
- Society
- Sports
- Technology
- Transportation
- Travel

When a selected topic has no recent chart, the card shows the newest chart
overall instead.

A hide-politics filter is on by default; turn it off if you want everything.
