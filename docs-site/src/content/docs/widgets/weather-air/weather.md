Current conditions, an hourly temperature trend line, and a multi-day forecast
strip, for anywhere in the world. US locations also get a National Weather
Service alert banner when a watch or warning is active.

**Tap the card** for the fuller picture: a wider forecast with more days and
more detail.

## Configure

**Settings → Weather.** Search any city worldwide, or type a 5-digit US ZIP.
Your location choice sets the unit automatically (US → °F, elsewhere → °C),
and the °F/°C toggle overrides it.

One location drives all three weather cards — this one,
[Air & Sky](/docs/widgets/weather-air/air-and-sky/) and
[Surf](/docs/widgets/weather-air/surf/).
