Amtrak departures from **Moynihan Train Hall / New York Penn**: minutes to
departure, route, train number, departure time, status, and track number when
one is assigned.

On a narrow card (four columns wide or less), each train shows its route and
number without the departure time, and its status only when the train is not
on time, for example "Acela 2151 · 5 min late". Wider cards and the full board
show both.

The card is filtered to your trip: it shows only trains that stop at your
chosen destination, with the arrival time there.

**Tap the card** for the full board.

## Configure

**Settings → Amtrak.** Pick your destination — it's required, named in the
card's corner, and the card prompts until you choose. Alerts can be toggled.
