idlescreen

A dashboard for your idle screen: your commute, your weather, your markets, your images.

Every widget is added and configured right on the board, or from your phone. 34 to choose from — take the ones you want.

Getting it on a board

Nothing is installed on the board. It loads idlescreen as its signage page, the way it would load any web address, so the setup is one address — https://idlescreen.app — and the handful of configurations that let the device show it. Which ones depends on what is on your wall. (A board already pointed at unsleep.app or roomboard.app keeps working: same dashboard, earlier names, and neither is going anywhere.)

Point it at the address

In Collaboration Control Hub, or the board’s own web interface: turn on the web engine and signage, allow touch, and give the board the address. It comes up on the welcome screen, ready for a setup code.

Or paste the macro

Optional. One macro sets every signage-friendly configuration in one go and adds a Dashboard button to the Control Panel. Non-touch Room series devices take the configuration in the address instead.

Making it yours

The board is a touchscreen: two buttons sit in the bottom-right corner, and the cards themselves are the third control. Rather set it up from your computer? idlescreen.app/setup lets you pick your widgets and hands you a code — on the board, tap the gear, choose Setup code, type it in, and Save. Codes last an hour.

Edit layout

Rearrange the board itself: drag cards to move them, resize them, remove one, or add a new widget from the picker. The board shows each card’s minimum size while you work, so nothing ends up too small to read. Tap Done when you’re finished.

Settings

Everything else: your display mode and schedule, screensaver sources, which stations, teams, tickers, and news outlets you follow, °F or °C, 12- or 24-hour time, and your display name.

Tap a card

A card that holds more than it shows opens full screen. Weather gives you the next 24 hours, Markets every ticker you follow, Subway the full alert text. A headline opens with a summary and a QR code so you can finish reading on your phone, and any picture fills the screen.

On a board

Screensavers

When the board is idle in Screensaver or Scheduled mode, it shows the full-screen scenes you’ve chosen instead of the widget grid. Pick one source; swipe the screen to advance it on demand.

Art, Landscapes

The public-domain masterpieces and the curated landscape collection, full screen.

iCloud Photos, Google Drive Photos

Your own albums, always shown whole, never cropped.

Big clock, World clocks, Clock + world times

One large face, analog dials for each of your World Clock cities, or a main clock with your world times beneath it.

Cities come from your World Clock settings.

Backdrop image: a scenic photo behind any of the three clock faces, changing once a day. Swipe to skip to the next one early.

Info strip: a slim strip along the bottom edge during screensavers showing weather and next trains. It can be turned off.
